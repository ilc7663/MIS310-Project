Understanding Your Digital Footprint

Your online activity creates a lasting picture of who you are. Learn what makes up your digital footprint, how it can affect your future, and what you can do to manage it.

Learn About Digital Footprints

Why Does Your Digital Footprint Matter?

Every time you post, comment, search, shop, or create an account online, you may leave behind information about yourself. Some of this information is shared intentionally, while some may be collected in the background. Over time, these pieces of information can become part of your digital identity.

For college students, understanding this matters because your online presence can become relevant when you apply for internships, jobs, graduate programs, or other professional opportunities.
