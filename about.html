WHO WE ARE

Helping students understand their digital footprint.

As a cybersecurity student interested in cybercrime and online safety, I wanted to help college students understand their digital footprint and why it matters.

ONLINE REPUTATION PRIVACY CYBERSECURITY FUTURE OPPORTUNITIES

01 / THE PURPOSE

Make digital footprints easier to understand.

Most of us spend a lot of time online, whether we're using social media, shopping, applying for jobs, commenting on posts, or simply browsing. Those activities can leave information behind and, over time, become part of our digital identity.

This website explains both the information we choose to share and the information collected as we use the internet. It offers college students useful guidance on online reputation, privacy, cybersecurity, and managing their presence over time.

Having a digital footprint doesn't mean you need to stop using social media or avoid being online. It's about being aware of what you share, who can see it, and what information you may leave behind.

02 / WHY IT MATTERS

Your online presence can shape first impressions.

Employers, colleges, and professional organizations may search for information about applicants online. Public posts, photos, comments, and other content can shape an impression before you meet.

“It takes 20 years to build a reputation but only a few minutes of a cyber incident to ruin it.”

Stéphane Nappo, cybersecurity professional

One post or photo doesn't define a person. The goal isn't to treat everything you do online as dangerous, but to understand the possible consequences and make informed choices that protect your reputation.

Your Digital Footprint Tells a Story

Every post, search, photo, and interaction becomes part of your online identity. Understanding that information can help you make more intentional choices.

04 / A FOOTPRINT CAN CHANGE

You're not stuck with your past.

Your digital footprint may be difficult to completely erase, but you can still manage it effectively and responsibly. An online presence is often unavoidable; what matters is deciding how you want to shape it.

Your digital footprint is part of your story. Make sure it's telling the story you want others to see.
