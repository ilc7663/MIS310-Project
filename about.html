About Us

Understanding Your Digital Footprint is an educational website created to help college students understand how their online presence can affect their future.

As a cybersecurity student interested in cybercrime and online safety, I created this site to raise awareness about the information people share and leave behind online. Social media posts, photos, comments, online accounts, and browsing activity can all contribute to a person's digital footprint and shape how others view them.

Our goal is to help students understand the potential impact of their digital footprint on employment, education, professional opportunities, and personal reputation. This website provides information, tips, and resources to help students make smarter decisions about what they share online.

Most importantly, having a digital footprint does not mean your online reputation is permanently out of your control. With awareness and responsible online habits, you can take steps to protect, manage, and improve your digital identity.

Learn about your digital footprint. Protect your online identity. Build a better digital future.
